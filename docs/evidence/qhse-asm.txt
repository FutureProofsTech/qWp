Observations:
- The public `verify_qhs1/2/3/31` wrappers are inlined; `verify_common` is the
  emitted shared verifier body.
- Calls are direct or GOT-indirect calls to named BLAKE3/hash helpers; there is
  no secret-dependent indirect dispatch.
- No `lfence`, `mfence`, `sfence`, or `ud2` instructions are emitted.
- The only branches are fixed-layout validation, loop control, and hash
  implementation control flow. Verification operates on public data.
- Speculative-execution fences are not injected: LFENCE is x86-specific, is not
  a general Spectre mitigation, and the verifier has no secret-dependent memory
  access. The signing code's constant-time behavior is measured separately with
  the Welch/dudect harnesses.
