leaf functions. They are outside the QHS-4.1 test filter and are covered by the
existing platform tests/evidence; they are not QHS-4.1 code.

byte-equivalence test (`sign_with_trees_matches_sequential`). The underlying
hash primitives are unchanged from QHS-4, whose targeted mutation run caught
93/93 non-equivalent viable mutants with 6 documented equivalents.
