qWp QHS-E Kani evidence
=======================

Date: 2026-11-10
Status: inherited parser/bounds coverage.

QHS-E is a verifier-only profile with exact-size public APIs. It does not add a
new parser or format. The QHS-1/2/3 parser and length proofs in `qwp-verify`
remain authoritative for the accepted formats:

- `qhs_public_key_parse_never_panics`
- `qhs_private_key_parse_never_panics`
- `qhs_signature_from_slice_checks_length`
- the QHS-2 and QHS-3 equivalents

QHS-E's fixed-size entry points, differential tests, wrong-length WOTS test,
attack suite, and fuzz run cover the profile-specific paths. A direct Kani proof
of the full verifier would require symbolically executing BLAKE3 chains and is
not tractable.
