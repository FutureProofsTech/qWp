# ADR-0004: QHS-3 High-Capacity Stateful Signatures

**Status:** Accepted — implementation in progress  
**Date:** 2026-11-06  
**Deciders:** qWp owner, crypto lead, red team  
**Related:** `docs/07-qhs2.md`, `docs/09-version-family.md`, `docs/03-formats.md`

---

## Context

QHS-2 reduces the signature to 1,704 bytes but remains limited to 256
signatures per key. High-volume release pipelines, CI signing, and multi-year
product lines need more signatures without giving up the compact hash-based
design or the 64-byte key format.

## Decision

QHS-3 keeps the QHS-2 WOTS parameters (base-64 digits, 45 chains of 63 steps)
and raises the Merkle height from 8 to 12:

- 4,096 leaves/signatures per key;
- 384-byte authentication path;
- 1,832-byte signature: `4 + 1,440 + 384 + 4`;
- 64-byte public and private keys;
- `next_leaf` remains `u16` and fits the existing 64-byte key layout;
- 131,140-byte public-leaf cache (128 KiB leaves).

QHS-1, QHS-2, and QHS-3.1 remain available; QHS-1 and QHS-2 are unchanged.

## Why h=12

- 4,096 signatures is enough for years of release/CI activity.
- Keygen remains under a second with parallel leaf derivation.
- The cache remains 128 KiB, practical for a host signer.
- The signature grows by only 128 bytes over QHS-2.
- h=16 would require a `u32` counter and a 2 MiB cache; it is deferred.

## Security

The proof is the QHS-2 reduction with 4,096 leaves instead of 256:

- WOTS reduction loss: `45 × 63 = 2,835`;
- theorem loss: `4,096 × 45 × 63 ≈ 2^21.47`;
- WOTS preimage margin: ≈234.5 bits;
- Merkle collision security: 128 bits;
- overall classical target: 128 bits;
- quantum target: at least 64 bits.

The stateful leaf invariant is unchanged: each signature consumes one of the
4,096 leaves, and the counter must be persisted atomically.

## Consequences

- New version/algorithm bytes `3/3`.
- New domain contexts `qWp QHS-3 ...`.
- New keyed-MAC cache header `[3, 3, 12, 2]` and fingerprint context.
- New `qhs3-*` CLI commands.
- New KAT, tests, attack harness, Kani proofs, fuzz coverage, and timing.
- A fresh 1-hour soak is required for the QHS-3 release candidate.
- QHS-1, QHS-2, and QHS-3.1 remain tagged/available.
