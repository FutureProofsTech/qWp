## 2. Assumptions accepted

1. BLAKE3 is preimage-resistant and collision-resistant for the fixed contexts.
2. BLAKE3 derive-key is a PRF for secret derivation.
3. The signer never reuses a leaf and never rolls back the counter.
4. The seed is generated by a CSPRNG (production keygen).
5. The signature checksum is integrity-only; authentication comes from WOTS + Merkle.

These assumptions are explicit. They are not hidden behind a standard or external audit.
